Nortlu - Privacy Policy Last updated: 16 October 2026 Nortlu is a private, personal assistant app used only by its owner, the creator LegosForU. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. What it accesses After the owner signs in and approves access, Nortlu reads the owner's own account information (profile name, follower counts, post and video statistics, and comments on the owner's own posts) and publishes the owner's own video clips to the owner's own accounts and Page. Comment replies are only posted on the owner's own posts. How data is stored and used All data stays on the owner's own computer. It is used only to show the owner their own statistics, to post the owner's own content, and to draft replies to comments on the owner's own posts. Sign-in tokens are encrypted on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected beyond the public comments on the owner's own posts, which are read only to draft replies. Deleting data Removing the app from your account settings (Facebook: Settings, Business Integrations; Instagram: Settings, Apps and websites) revokes all access, and disconnecting an account in Nortlu deletes its stored sign-in from the owner's computer. To ask about data deletion, open an issue at github.com/cyleburkhalter428/nortlu-legal/issues.
